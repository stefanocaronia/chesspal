# The piece sets

Chesspal's three sets are its own, drawn with Codex and traced into vectors for Chesspal:

- **Nitida**, original pieces;
- **Torretta**, original pieces;
- **Capablanca**, after the diagrams of J. R. Capablanca's *Chess Fundamentals* (Harcourt, Brace and Company, 1921), a book in the public domain:
  the classic shapes of the chess books, redrawn.

Each set also has its small pieces, `<set>-small.svg`, for the pieces taken and the moves: each piece reduced to one solid silhouette. Each file
names its author and licence in its first comment.
