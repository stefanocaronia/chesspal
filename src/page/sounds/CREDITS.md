# The board's sounds

The sounds of the board page were recorded for Chesspal by Stefano Caronia; they are under Chesspal's licence. Each is a short Ogg Vorbis file, mono at
48 kHz, named after the moment it plays.

| Sound | Plays for | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `move.ogg` | a piece set down | Stefano Caronia for Chesspal | MIT, as Chesspal |
| `capture.ogg` | a capture | Stefano Caronia for Chesspal | MIT, as Chesspal |
| `check.ogg` | a check | Stefano Caronia for Chesspal | MIT, as Chesspal |
| `mate.ogg` | a mate | Stefano Caronia for Chesspal | MIT, as Chesspal |
| `draw.ogg` | a draw, stalemate and repetition too | Stefano Caronia for Chesspal | MIT, as Chesspal |
